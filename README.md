# PP LLC CarCam — Product Management Portfolio

## Overview

This portfolio presents a product management case study for **PP LLC CarCam**, a connected vehicle camera product designed to provide drivers with enhanced visibility, recording, and safety-related capabilities.

The project demonstrates my approach to **product discovery, requirements definition, customer-focused product development, prioritization, Agile project management, and AI-assisted product analysis**.

## Product Management Focus

The project explores the product lifecycle from an initial business and customer need through product definition and delivery planning.

Key areas include:

* Product vision and objectives
* Customer needs and problem definition
* User personas and use cases
* Product requirements
* User stories and acceptance criteria
* Feature prioritization
* Agile/Scrum planning
* Product roadmap and delivery considerations
* Risk and stakeholder analysis
* AI-assisted research and product analysis
* Business and operational considerations

## My Approach

I approached the CarCam project from the perspective of a **product manager connecting customer needs, business objectives, and implementation requirements**.

The project emphasizes translating business and customer problems into actionable product requirements while maintaining a practical focus on value, feasibility, prioritization, and continuous improvement.

## Portfolio Deliverables

The repository contains supporting documentation demonstrating different stages of the product-management process, including product analysis, requirements, Agile planning, and project deliverables.

Each document provides additional detail about the decisions, methods, and outputs developed during the project.
